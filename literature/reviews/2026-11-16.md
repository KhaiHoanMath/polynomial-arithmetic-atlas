### Dumas version warning

In v1 Theorem 3, take \(K=\mathbb Q\), \(v=v_2\), and
\[
f=(x^2-1)^2/2=x^4/2-x^2+1/2.
\]
For \(n=4,j=2\), the coefficient valuations are \((-1,\infty,0,\infty,-1)\). The displayed inequalities hold and \(d_1=d_2=2\), yet all irreducible factors have degree one. Version 2 changes the lower-degree statements: its Theorem 4(iii), at \(i=0\), would require \(1\le-1\), excluding this example. The arXiv history explicitly notes corrections. **This is a counterexample to the inspected v1 formulation only**, not an allegation about the journal article. The publisher full text was inaccessible; v2's complete proof has not been verified. Do not import the v1 bound into `eisenstein-dumas` or `newton-polygons`.

Sources: <https://arxiv.org/pdf/2511.21427v1>, <https://arxiv.org/pdf/2511.21427v2>, <https://arxiv.org/abs/2511.21427>, <https://doi.org/10.1007/s11587-026-01203-8>.

### Orbital arboreal revision

The v4 local theorem imposes \(p\nmid d\) and distinguishes projective fibers from affine fibers with the additional condition \(\bar x\ne\widetilde\varphi^{,n}(\infty)\). Section 4.1 explicitly corrects the ambient group: the full automorphism group of the unrooted \((d+1)\)-regular tree is locally compact, and the paper uses a spine-fixing subgroup as its profinite target. This should not be confused with the atlas's classical rooted-tree groups. The correction and definitions were inspected; the broader equivalence and proofs remain deferred. The collector's journal DOI was not checked against a publisher full text.

Source: <https://arxiv.org/pdf/2510.23097v4>.
